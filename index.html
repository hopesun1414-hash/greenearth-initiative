Why conservation matters

A healthier planet for generations to come

Nature supports our lives through clean air, fresh water, healthy soil and habitats for wildlife. Protecting it requires action from individuals and communities alike.

GreenEarth Initiative is a demonstration project focused on environmental education, habitat restoration, responsible waste management and climate awareness.
